Gmail as a Kanban board. Here is everything it does so far.

51features as of November 4, 2026

Board and columns

9
  1. 1Gmail shown as a Kanban board, with an Inbox column and label-based columns
  2. 2Add, rename and remove columns
  3. 3Use your existing Gmail labels as columns, with a picker on first sign-in
  4. 4New users start with no columns, and no labels are added until they add one
  5. 5Resizable columns with a drag grip
  6. 6Double-click a column header to minimize it to a vertical strip, which still accepts dropped cards
  7. 7Sort each column by newest, oldest, unread first, sender or subject
  8. 8Color coding for cards
  9. 9Nest an email under another email, like an outline, and collapse or expand the nest. Nests stay when you reload and move together

Moving and managing email

10
  1. 10Drag cards between columns, with a see-through mail icon following the pointer
  2. 11Moving a card to a column keeps it in your Gmail Inbox
  3. 12Archive
  4. 13Delete by pressing Delete twice while hovering over a card, with Esc or a short wait to cancel
  5. 14Select several cards, or a whole column, for bulk actions
  6. 15Close, Delete All: delete everything in a column and close it
  7. 16Delete forever from Trash
  8. 17Undo for moves and deletes, with Ctrl+Z, a button and the on-screen message
  9. 18Confirmation dialogs that resist accidental presses, with Cancel focused and a brief delay on OK
  10. 19Keyboard control: arrow keys, 1–9 to move a card, Enter to open, Esc to close

Snooze and new mail

7
  1. 20Snooze emails, including picking a date and time
  2. 21Snoozed emails come back unread at the top of the Inbox
  3. 22Separate sidebar folders for Gmail's snoozes and StediMail's
  4. 23Optional Google helper script that wakes snoozes while StediMail is closed
  5. 24Returned emails move to the top of the Inbox
  6. 25New mail appears by itself, checked every 15 seconds
  7. 26The Inbox jumps to the top for new mail, but waits until your mouse rests

Reading

8
  1. 27Email reader panel that you can widen
  2. 28Newest message open at the top, with earlier ones tucked behind a button
  3. 29Each message shows its subject, and the title bar stays visible with an Esc hint
  4. 30Pictures inside emails display inline
  5. 31Emails always show in their light version, so dark-mode text doesn't vanish
  6. 32Copy button when you hover over an email address
  7. 33Open in Gmail button
  8. 34Unsubscribe button, including one-click, which also trashes the email with Undo available

Writing

5
  1. 35Compose and reply in a rich editor: bold, italic, underline, lists, attachments, links and emoji
  2. 36Reply and Reply all, with Reply all as the default only when there are other people on the thread
  3. 37Send + Archive
  4. 38Unsent replies and new messages are saved as Gmail drafts and restored
  5. 39Recipient suggestions from your Google Contacts and past emails

Search and sidebar

6
  1. 40Gmail-style search, with recent searches, quick filters and Tab completion
  2. 41“Did you mean” suggestions when a search finds nothing
  3. 42Folder sidebar with unread counts
  4. 43Create, edit and remove labels and sublabels, and show or hide them
  5. 44Deleting a label returns its emails to the Inbox first
  6. 45Button that opens your Gmail rules

Accounts, testers and accessibility

6
  1. 46Built-in Google sign-in, with quiet renewal before it expires
  2. 47Separate settings for each Gmail account in the same browser
  3. 48Install as an app, including on phones, with a single-column phone view
  4. 49Feedback button that emails a problem or request to beta@stedimail.com and never includes email content
  5. 50Mail goes only between your browser and Google, with no StediMail server, and the page's security settings limit its connections to Google
  6. 51Larger click targets and cards that accessibility tools can recognize, for easier aiming
StediMail is in testing, and this list grows as it does. Feedback goes to beta@stedimail.com.
